Spry Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

The short version
  • Your camera video is processed on your phone and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your details, scan results and routine history are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • We have no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell or share your information.
  • Spry is free and has no in-app purchases, so no payment or subscription service is used.

Who we are

Spry is a mobility app made by Muochu Hu ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, email us at parwaiznj@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

To personalize your scan and routine, Spry stores the following in a private database inside the app on your phone:

  • Your first name, birth year and sex.
  • Your answers to the safety questions.
  • Your scan results, such as how many chair stands you did and your Spry Age.
  • Which days you finished your routine, and your reminder settings.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We do not have a server that stores it, and we cannot access it.

Your camera

During a scan, Spry uses your camera to measure how you move. Each video frame is analysed on your phone to find the position of your body, and then discarded. Video and images are never recorded, saved or uploaded.

To do this, the app downloads a body-tracking model the first time you scan. It is downloaded from jsDelivr (a content delivery network) and Google Cloud Storage. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic technical information about the request. No camera images or personal details are sent to them.

Purchases and subscriptions

Right now: Spry is free. It has no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to any payment or subscription service.

If we add subscriptions later: we will update this policy before they go live, and tell you in the app. The rest of this section explains how they would work.

If you subscribe, payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email address or payment card details.

We would use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat would receive:

  • An anonymous ID created by the app. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Your purchase and subscription records from Apple or Google.
  • Basic device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, country or region, and IP address.

RevenueCat would not receive your scan results, profile details or camera data. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

App updates

Spry checks for small updates using Expo's update service. When it checks, it sends technical information such as your device platform, the app version and your IP address. You can read Expo's privacy policy.

Reminders and voice

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not use push notification services or collect notification tokens. Spoken instructions use your phone's built-in voice.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information.

Health information

Your safety answers and scan results relate to your movement and wellbeing. They are used only inside the app, on your phone, to choose your scan tests and daily routine. Spry is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and this information is not a medical record.

Keeping and deleting your information

Information on your phone stays there until you delete it. In Settings, "Delete all my data" erases your details, scans, routine history and settings at once, and deleting the app does the same. "Log out" removes your details and keeps your scan history on the phone. You can change your details at any time by choosing "Update my details" in Settings.

Once subscriptions are offered, subscription records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept according to their own policies. To ask for your RevenueCat record to be deleted, email us and include the approximate date you subscribed.

Children

Spry is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Because your information is stored on your phone, you can view, change or delete it directly in the app, or by deleting the app. For anything held by our service providers, email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
