The scan

How do I set up my phone for the scan?

Prop your phone up at about waist height, about 2 metres (6 feet) away, so it can see your whole body from head to feet. Have a sturdy chair behind you and a counter or wall within reach. Good lighting helps. Spry will talk you through each step.

The app says it can't see me. What should I do?

Step back until your head and feet are both in view. Make sure the room is well lit and nothing is between you and the phone. Wearing clothes that contrast with the background also helps.

Why was the balance test skipped?

If you answered "yes" to any safety question, Spry skips the one-leg balance test and keeps your routine gentle. You can change your answers at any time with "Update my details" on the home screen. Please check with your doctor before starting new exercise.

What is my Spry Age?

It compares how you move with typical results for people of your age and sex. It is a wellness estimate, not a medical test, and it can't diagnose any condition. Re-scan every week or so to see how it changes.

Privacy

Is my camera video recorded or uploaded?

No. Video is analysed on your phone as you move and is never saved or sent anywhere. Your details and results are also stored only on your phone. See our privacy policy.

How do I delete my information?

Everything is stored on your phone. To erase it, open Settings in Spry and choose "Delete all my data", or delete the app. "Log out" removes your details but keeps your scan history on the phone. To change your details instead, choose "Update my details" in Settings.

Cost

Is Spry free?

Yes. Everything in Spry is free, including the scan, your results and your daily routine. There are no in-app purchases.

Will it stay free?

The scan and your results will always be free. Later, we may offer a subscription for the daily routine. We will tell you in the app before that happens, and you will never be charged unless you choose to subscribe.

Reminders

How do I turn reminders on, off or change the time?

Open Settings in the app. You can turn the daily routine reminder and the weekly re-scan reminder on or off, and move the time earlier or later. If reminders don't appear, check that notifications are allowed for Spry in your phone's settings.

Safety

Spry is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Talk to your doctor before starting new exercise. Stop any test or exercise that causes pain or makes you feel unwell. If you feel chest pain, severe dizziness or shortness of breath, stop and get medical help right away.
